## Residual risks
1. **S1 is a hyperlipidaemia diet guide from mainland China, not a post-PCI or Hong Kong guideline.** We use it because most post-PCI patients have high lipids, and because it is the most authoritative published source for TCM food therapy (食养). Recipes containing 当归 or 桃仁 are deliberately excluded because the patient takes dual antiplatelet therapy.
2. **The S1 link is a copy hosted by a medical college.** To confirm the original, search nhc.gov.cn for notice 国卫办食品函〔2023〕5号.
3. **Herb–drug bleeding evidence (S8, S9, S11) is mostly case reports, lab studies and observational data.** The app therefore says "avoid unless prescribed" or "ask your doctor", never "dangerous".
4. **Soma sensor accuracy has no external source.** It is a design target to be validated in our Step 4 study.
5. **All patient data in the app is simulated.**
